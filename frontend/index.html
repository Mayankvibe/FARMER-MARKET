ℹ️ How recommendations work

The system takes your crop and compares: (1) the highest offered price from all active buyer requests, and (2) the highest current modal price from the mandi data CSV. If the buyer offers more, you're recommended to sell to the buyer. Otherwise, go to the mandi. This is a rule-based comparison — simple, transparent, and explainable.
